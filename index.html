Approach

How an engagement runs

The same four stages apply whether the work is a short advisory engagement or a multi-month software build.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We define the problem precisely — the technical, operational, and business constraints a solution will need to work within.

  2. 02

    Research & design

    Where the engagement calls for it, we research the relevant technical approaches and evaluate them against those constraints.

  3. 03

    Build

    Systems are developed against the agreed specification, with review points scheduled through the engagement rather than a single delivery at the end.

  4. 04

    Deploy & support

    We support deployment into the client's environment and remain available for subsequent iterations and maintenance.
